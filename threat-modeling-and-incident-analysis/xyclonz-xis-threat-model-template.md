# Xyclonz Information System (XIS) — Threat Model

## 1. Objectives (5)
1. Protect confidentiality and integrity of genomic and patient clinical/lifestyle datasets.
2. Maintain availability and integrity of data collection, storage, and processing pipelines.
3. Enforce role-based access and auditability across internal researchers and external data feeds.
4. Preserve partner trust and regulatory compliance for rare-disease research workflows.
5. Support resilient, cost-controlled system operations prior to full production build.
